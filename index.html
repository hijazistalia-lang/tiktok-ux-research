01 · Product research

StarScore

User feedbackMazeTypeformUsability analysisFigma

Making the app clearer and more enjoyable to use.

The original interface felt bland, and customers did not always understand what the tabs did or how the features were different. I used their feedback to focus the redesign on navigation, labels, visual hierarchy, and clearer feature presentation.

Open the StarScore Figma project ↗
01 / FRAME

Research question

Why were customers struggling to understand and distinguish the product’s features?

02 / LISTEN

Collect feedback

Reviewed customer responses about the tabs, visual appeal, and the overall journey.

03 / SYNTHESIZE

Identify patterns

Grouped repeated concerns about feature purpose, hierarchy, distinction, and unclear paths.

04 / DESIGN

Apply the findings

Reworked the navigation, tab distinction, interface hierarchy, and feature presentation in Figma.

05 / EVALUATE

Measure response

Compared reported customer satisfaction before and after the redesign.

+28percentage point increase
Before redesign56%
After redesign84%

This result comes from customer satisfaction feedback collected before and after the redesign. I will add the participant count when I recover the original study record.

What I learned

The problem was not only visual appeal. Customers needed a clearer understanding of what each tab and feature was for.

What I changed

I created stronger hierarchy, clearer feature identities, and more direct paths through the app.

What happened

Reported satisfaction increased from 56% to 84%. Customers also reported clearer feature distinction and a more understandable journey.

02 · Market research

Kairos

Survey designInterviewsSegmentationMarket analysisProduct strategy

Learning which audience Kairos should serve first.

Kairos was exploring several directions at once, including a professional chart tool, an interactive horoscope, a personal reference notebook, and a future social platform. I researched who the users were, what they valued, how often they practiced astrology, and which features mattered most.

Open the Kairos Figma project ↗
01 / DEFINE

Business problem

The app was trying to serve too many purposes without a clearly prioritized audience.

02 / RECRUIT

Two channels

Reached existing subscribers and broader Reddit and Discord communities.

03 / MEASURE

Survey behavior

Asked about experience, frequency, desired features, frustrations, and social engagement.

04 / COMPARE

Compare needs

Compared the professional needs of advanced users with the interests of more casual users.

05 / RECOMMEND

Guide direction

Used the patterns to recommend product features, positioning, usability improvements, and marketing opportunities.

What I recommended

The clearest direction was to serve experienced users first. They wanted professional tools, interactive charts, better organization, and clearer navigation. Social publishing and general horoscope content could be added later without distracting from the main professional experience. Instagram also appeared to be the best shared place to reach both audiences. Since the sample was small, I recommended more research before making a permanent roadmap decision.

Study limits: 19 total responses, different recruitment channels, likely subscriber bias, and some skipped questions. These percentages describe the people who responded and are not estimates for the full market.
